Business Rules: Customer Loan Eligibility

1. A customer is eligible to apply for a personal loan only if the customer age is between 21 and 60 years.
2. The customer must have a monthly income of at least 30000.
3. The requested loan amount must be between 50000 and 1000000.
4. If the customer credit score is below 650, the loan application must be rejected.
5. If mandatory customer details are missing, the application must not be submitted.
6. The system must calculate monthly EMI based on loan amount, interest rate, and tenure.
7. Tenure must be between 12 and 60 months.
